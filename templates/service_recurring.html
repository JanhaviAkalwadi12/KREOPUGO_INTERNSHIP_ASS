    Recurring Deposit

    Save regularly, build financial discipline and create a secure fund for your future goals.

    What is a Recurring Deposit?

    A Recurring Deposit (RD) is a savings scheme that allows you to deposit a fixed amount every month for a selected period. It is designed for customers who want to develop a regular and disciplined saving habit.

    Instead of depositing a large amount at once, customers can make smaller monthly deposits. At the end of the selected tenure, the accumulated amount along with applicable interest is paid as the maturity amount.

    Key Features

    • Fixed monthly deposit amount.
    • Flexible deposit tenure as per applicable bank rules.
    • Earn interest on your regular savings.
    • Encourages disciplined financial planning.
    • Suitable for short and medium-term goals.
    • Easy and convenient way to save regularly.

    Benefits

    • Develops a regular saving habit.
    • Helps plan funds for future expenses.
    • Suitable for students and working individuals.
    • Provides an opportunity to earn interest.
    • Useful for education, travel and other planned expenses.
    • Helps achieve financial goals systematically.

    Eligibility

    • Individuals eligible to open an account with the bank.
    • Students, subject to applicable rules.
    • Salaried employees.
    • Self-employed individuals.
    • Joint account holders, wherever permitted.
    • Other eligible customers as per bank guidelines.

    Documents Required

    • Valid identity proof.
    • Valid address proof.
    • Recent passport-size photograph.
    • Completed RD account opening form.
    • Additional documents, if required by the bank.

    Why Choose a Recurring Deposit?

    A Recurring Deposit is useful when you want to save a fixed amount regularly instead of making a large one-time investment. By depositing every month, you can gradually build a fund while earning applicable interest on your savings.

    Ideal For

    • Monthly income earners
    • Students planning future expenses
    • Families building planned savings
    • Customers preparing for education expenses
    • Customers planning travel or other purchases

    Things to Remember

    • Deposit the agreed amount regularly.
    • Choose a tenure suitable for your financial goal.
    • Interest rates are subject to applicable bank rules.
    • Premature closure may be subject to applicable terms.
    • Actual maturity value depends on the applicable scheme.

    Important Information

    Interest rates, minimum deposit amount, tenure, premature withdrawal conditions and other terms are subject to the bank's prevailing rules and policies. Please contact the bank for the latest applicable terms before opening an account.
