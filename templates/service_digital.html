    Digital Banking

    Convenient and secure access to banking services through supported digital channels.

    About Digital Banking

    Digital banking allows customers to access selected banking services through electronic and online channels. It provides a convenient way to manage accounts, check transaction information and perform eligible banking activities without always having to visit a branch.

    Key Features

    • Convenient access to selected banking services
    • Access to account information through digital channels
    • Helps customers monitor transactions
    • Supports eligible online banking activities
    • Available through supported electronic channels

    Benefits

    • Reduces the need for frequent branch visits
    • Saves time for routine banking activities
    • Provides convenient access to account information
    • Makes everyday banking more accessible
    • Supports modern banking habits

    Digital Banking Services

    Depending on the facilities offered by the bank, customers may be able to access services such as account balance checking, transaction history, fund transfers, bill payments, account statements and other eligible banking facilities through supported digital channels.

    Account Services

    • Check available account information
    • View transaction history
    • Access account statements
    • Monitor deposits and withdrawals
    • Manage eligible account services

    Online Transactions

    • Make eligible fund transfers
    • Manage selected online payments
    • Access supported payment services
    • Review transaction details
    • Receive applicable transaction notifications

    Banking at Your Convenience

    Digital banking makes routine banking activities more convenient by providing access through supported electronic channels. Customers can manage many everyday banking requirements from their preferred location, subject to the services and facilities provided by the bank.

    Safe and Secure Banking

    Customers should use only official banking applications, websites and other authorized channels when accessing digital banking services. Login details, passwords, PINs, OTPs and other confidential banking information should be kept private and should never be shared with other people.

    Who Can Use Digital Banking?

    Customers who maintain eligible accounts and meet the applicable requirements of the bank may use supported digital banking services. Availability of individual facilities may depend on account type, registration and other applicable conditions.

    Basic Requirements

    • Eligible bank account
    • Registered customer information
    • Registered mobile number where applicable
    • Suitable device and internet connection
    • Registration for applicable digital services

    Customer Responsibilities

    • Keep login information confidential
    • Use official banking channels
    • Check transaction details regularly
    • Report suspicious activity promptly
    • Follow applicable banking security guidelines

    Advantages of Digital Banking

    Digital banking can make routine financial activities faster and more convenient. It provides customers with easier access to account information and selected banking services while reducing dependence on branch visits for certain everyday requirements.

    Important Information

    Availability of digital banking services, transaction limits, charges, authentication methods, service timings and other conditions may vary according to the bank and the type of service. Customers should use only authorized banking channels and confirm the current service details with the bank.

    Project Information

    This webpage is created as part of an educational banking website project. The information presented is for demonstration purposes and does not represent an actual banking service, offer or contractual commitment by the bank.
