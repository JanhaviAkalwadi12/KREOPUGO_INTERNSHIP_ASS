    Current Account

    A convenient banking solution designed for businesses and customers with regular transactions.

    About Current Account

    A current account is a banking facility mainly designed for businesses, professionals and customers who need to carry out frequent financial transactions. It provides convenient access to banking services for managing business receipts, payments and other regular financial activities.

    Key Features

    • Designed for frequent financial transactions
    • Suitable for business and professional requirements
    • Convenient deposit and withdrawal facilities
    • Helps manage regular business payments
    • Supports organized transaction management

    Benefits

    • Helps manage regular business transactions
    • Makes business payments more convenient
    • Helps maintain financial records
    • Supports day-to-day business activities
    • Provides convenient access to applicable banking services

    Purpose of a Current Account

    A current account is intended for customers who need frequent banking transactions. It can be useful for receiving business payments, making payments to suppliers, managing operating expenses and maintaining regular business-related financial activities.

    Business Transactions

    • Receiving payments from customers
    • Making payments to suppliers
    • Managing regular business expenses
    • Handling eligible business transactions
    • Maintaining transaction records

    Account Management

    • Monitoring account activity
    • Reviewing deposits and withdrawals
    • Maintaining proper financial records
    • Managing regular payments
    • Using applicable banking channels

    Who Can Open a Current Account?

    Businesses, firms, professionals, traders and other eligible customers who require frequent financial transactions may open a current account, subject to the applicable account-opening requirements and policies of the bank.

    Eligibility Requirements

    Eligibility may depend on the nature of the business or professional activity, customer identification, required documentation and other conditions specified by the bank. Applicants must satisfy the applicable requirements before opening the account.

    Documents Required

    • Identity proof of the applicant
    • Address proof
    • Business or professional details
    • Registration or constitution documents, where applicable
    • Other documents as required by the bank

    Suitable For

    • Small and medium businesses
    • Traders and business owners
    • Professionals
    • Firms and eligible organizations
    • Customers requiring frequent transactions

    Convenient Banking Access

    Depending on the facilities offered by the bank, current account customers may be able to access selected services through branch and supported digital banking channels. These services can help customers monitor transactions and manage routine banking activities more conveniently.

    Transaction Management

    A current account can help businesses maintain a structured record of their financial transactions. Regular monitoring of receipts, payments, deposits and withdrawals can support better organization of day-to-day business finances.

    Importance for Businesses

    A current account can provide a convenient way for businesses to manage their routine financial activities. It helps separate business transactions from personal financial activities and supports organized management of business-related payments and receipts.

    Important Information

    Minimum balance requirements, transaction limits, charges, available facilities, documentation and other account conditions may vary according to the type of current account and the bank's applicable policies. Customers should confirm the current details with the bank.
