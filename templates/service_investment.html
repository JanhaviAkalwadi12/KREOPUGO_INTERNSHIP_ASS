    Investment Services

    Plan your financial future with secure investment options designed to help you achieve your financial goals.

    What are Investment Services?

    Investment services help customers plan and manage their savings according to their financial goals. By selecting suitable investment options, customers can work towards building financial security for their future.

    Anish Souharda Cooperative Bank provides different savings and investment solutions to support customers in planning for education, home purchase, retirement, family needs and other long-term financial objectives.

    Investment Options

    • Fixed Deposit investment.
    • Recurring Deposit savings.
    • Goal-based savings plans.
    • Long-term financial planning.
    • Senior citizen investment options.
    • Other investment schemes as per bank rules.

    Benefits

    • Helps build disciplined savings.
    • Supports long-term financial planning.
    • Provides opportunities to earn applicable returns.
    • Helps achieve planned financial goals.
    • Suitable for different financial requirements.
    • Encourages better money management.

    Who Can Invest?

    • Individuals eligible to open an account.
    • Students, subject to applicable rules.
    • Salaried employees.
    • Self-employed individuals.
    • Senior citizens.
    • Other eligible customers as per bank guidelines.

    Documents Required

    • Valid identity proof.
    • Valid address proof.
    • Recent passport-size photograph.
    • Completed account or investment form.
    • Bank account details, wherever required.
    • Additional documents as required by the bank.

    Why Choose Investment Services?

    Investment planning helps you use your money effectively instead of keeping all your savings unused. Choosing an appropriate investment option can help you prepare for future financial needs while maintaining a disciplined approach to saving.

    Suitable For

    • Education planning
    • Home purchase planning
    • Retirement planning
    • Family financial requirements
    • Future emergency funds
    • Long-term financial goals

    Things to Remember

    • Choose an investment according to your financial goal.
    • Consider the investment period before investing.
    • Understand the applicable terms and conditions.
    • Interest or returns depend on the selected scheme.
    • Premature withdrawal may be subject to applicable rules.
    • Review your financial requirements before investing.

    Important Information

    Interest rates, investment periods, minimum deposit amounts, withdrawal conditions and other terms are subject to the bank's prevailing rules and policies. Please contact the bank for the latest applicable terms and conditions before making an investment.
