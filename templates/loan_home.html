    Home Loan

    Turn your dream of owning a home into reality with suitable home loan solutions designed to support your housing needs.

    What is a Home Loan?

    A home loan is a financial facility that helps eligible customers meet the cost of purchasing, constructing or improving a residential property, subject to the bank's applicable policies.

    Anish Souharda Cooperative Bank provides home loan facilities to support eligible customers in achieving their residential housing goals while allowing them to repay the approved loan amount through suitable instalments.

    Loan Purpose

    • Purchase of an eligible residential property.
    • Construction of a residential house.
    • Purchase of a residential plot, where applicable.
    • Home improvement or renovation, where applicable.
    • Other approved housing requirements.

    Benefits

    • Helps make home ownership more achievable.
    • Supports eligible housing requirements.
    • Structured repayment through instalments.
    • Suitable loan options based on eligibility.
    • Helps manage the cost of a home over time.
    • Assistance from bank officials during processing.

    Eligibility

    • Eligible Indian citizens.
    • Salaried individuals.
    • Self-employed individuals.
    • Business owners, where applicable.
    • Applicants with suitable repayment capacity.
    • Other eligible customers as per bank guidelines.

    Documents Required

    • Valid identity proof.
    • Valid address proof.
    • Recent passport-size photographs.
    • Income and financial documents.
    • Property-related documents, wherever required.
    • Bank account and other supporting documents.

    Why Choose a Home Loan?

    Buying or constructing a home is an important financial decision. A home loan can help eligible customers manage the cost of their housing requirement while repaying the approved amount through a planned repayment schedule.

    Home Loan Features

    • Finance for eligible residential requirements.
    • Repayment through regular instalments.
    • Loan amount based on eligibility and requirement.
    • Repayment period according to applicable rules.
    • Property assessment as required.
    • Terms based on the bank's prevailing policies.

    Suitable For

    • First-time home buyers.
    • Customers purchasing a residential property.
    • Customers constructing a house.
    • Eligible home renovation requirements.
    • Families planning residential property ownership.
    • Other approved housing requirements.

    Home Loan EMI

    The monthly EMI depends on factors such as the approved loan amount, applicable interest rate and repayment period. Customers should select a repayment plan that is suitable for their financial capacity.

    Things to Remember

    • Choose a property according to your budget.
    • Check your repayment capacity before applying.
    • Understand the applicable interest rate.
    • Review the loan tenure carefully.
    • Keep property documents ready.
    • Read all loan terms and conditions carefully.

    Application Process

    • Submit the completed home loan application.
    • Provide identity and address documents.
    • Submit income and financial details.
    • Provide required property documents.
    • The bank verifies eligibility and documents.
    • Loan approval is subject to applicable conditions.

    Important Information

    Interest rates, loan amounts, repayment periods, processing charges, property requirements, security conditions and eligibility criteria are subject to the bank's prevailing rules and policies. Please contact the bank for the latest applicable terms and conditions before applying.
