    Savings Account

    A simple and convenient banking solution to help customers save, manage and access their money.

    About Savings Account

    A savings account is a basic banking facility designed to help customers safely deposit their money, develop regular saving habits and manage their everyday financial requirements. It provides convenient access to account services while helping customers maintain their savings in an organized manner.

    Key Features

    • Convenient account management
    • Suitable for regular personal savings
    • Easy access to account information
    • Supports regular banking transactions
    • Helps maintain organized financial records

    Benefits

    • Encourages regular saving habits
    • Helps customers manage their money
    • Convenient for everyday banking needs
    • Helps organize personal finances
    • Provides access to applicable banking facilities

    Purpose of a Savings Account

    A savings account can be used to keep money safely, build regular savings and manage day-to-day financial requirements. It can also help customers keep track of their deposits, withdrawals and other eligible account transactions.

    Everyday Banking

    • Deposit money into the account
    • Withdraw funds when required
    • Review account transactions
    • Maintain personal financial records
    • Access applicable banking services

    Saving Habits

    • Encourages regular saving
    • Helps plan personal finances
    • Supports disciplined money management
    • Helps maintain funds for future needs
    • Makes personal savings easier to organize

    Who Can Open a Savings Account?

    Individuals and other eligible customers who wish to maintain personal savings and use applicable banking facilities may open a savings account, subject to the bank's account-opening requirements and applicable eligibility conditions.

    Documents Required

    • Valid identity proof
    • Address proof
    • Required photographs
    • Customer identification details
    • Other documents as required by the bank

    Account Management

    • Regular monitoring of account activity
    • Maintaining sufficient account balance
    • Reviewing transaction records
    • Keeping account information updated
    • Using applicable banking channels

    Convenient Banking Access

    Depending on the facilities provided by the bank, savings account customers may be able to access selected banking services through branch and supported digital channels. These facilities can make account management, transaction checking and other routine banking activities more convenient.

    Suitable For

    A savings account is suitable for individuals who want to save money, manage everyday financial requirements, maintain an organized account and access applicable banking facilities according to their needs.

    Important Information

    Account-opening requirements, minimum balance conditions, interest rates, charges, transaction limits, facilities and other terms may vary according to the type of account and the bank's applicable policies. Customers should confirm the current details with the bank.
