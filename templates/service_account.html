    Account Statements

    Easy access to account transaction records for better financial tracking and management.

    About Account Statements

    An Account Statement is a record of the financial transactions carried out through a customer's bank account during a selected period. It provides important information about deposits, withdrawals, transfers, payments and the available account balance.

    Account statements help customers monitor their account activity, keep track of transactions and maintain proper financial records. They can be obtained through supported banking channels such as branches, internet banking and mobile banking.

    Key Features

    • Detailed record of account transactions
    • Transaction date and description
    • Deposit and withdrawal information
    • Transfer and payment records
    • Opening and closing balance details
    • Statements for selected periods

    Benefits

    • Helps track financial activity
    • Makes transaction verification easier
    • Supports personal financial management
    • Useful for maintaining financial records
    • Helps identify unfamiliar transactions
    • Convenient access to past transactions

    Purpose of Account Statements

    The main purpose of an account statement is to provide customers with a clear record of transactions made through their account. It helps customers understand how money has been credited, debited and transferred during a particular period.

    Information Included

    • Account holder details
    • Account number or masked account information
    • Transaction dates
    • Transaction descriptions
    • Credit and debit amounts
    • Available or closing balance

    Transaction Details

    • Cash deposits
    • Cash withdrawals
    • Fund transfers
    • Electronic payments
    • Interest or other account entries where applicable

    Ways to Access Account Statements

    Customers may be able to access their account statements through different banking channels. The available options depend on the services provided by the bank and the type of account maintained by the customer.

    Bank Branch

    • Request a statement through the branch
    • Customer verification may be required
    • Suitable for customers who prefer branch assistance
    • Statements may be provided in an approved format

    Digital Banking

    • Access statements through supported online services
    • View recent account activity
    • Select available statement periods
    • Download or save statements where supported

    Statement Period

    An account statement can generally be requested for a particular period, depending on the banking facility. Customers may choose a suitable period such as recent transactions or a longer historical period where records are available.

    Who Can Request a Statement?

    • Eligible account holders
    • Joint account holders as permitted
    • Authorised persons where applicable
    • Customers using approved banking channels

    Basic Requirements

    • Active bank account
    • Customer identification or authentication
    • Required account details
    • Selected statement period
    • Access to the chosen banking channel

    Importance of Account Statements

    Account statements are useful for maintaining an organised record of financial transactions. Customers can use them to review their income, expenses, transfers and other account activities.

    Regularly checking account statements can also help customers notice unexpected or unfamiliar transactions and contact the bank through appropriate channels when clarification is required.

    Financial Management

    • Track income and expenses
    • Review spending patterns
    • Monitor account balances
    • Maintain organised financial records
    • Support personal budgeting

    Other Uses

    • Reference for past transactions
    • Supporting financial documentation where accepted
    • Business record keeping
    • Transaction verification
    • Personal financial planning

    Privacy and Security

    Account statements contain financial information and should be handled carefully. Customers should avoid sharing statements unnecessarily and should protect downloaded or printed copies from unauthorised access.

    When using digital banking, customers should use secure devices and authorised banking channels to access their account information.

    Digital Account Statements

    Digital statements provide a convenient way to view and maintain account records without depending entirely on printed documents. Where supported, customers can access statements online and save them for future reference.

    Digital access can reduce paperwork and make transaction information available more conveniently to customers.

    Important Information

    Statement availability, statement periods, formats, charges and access methods may vary depending on the bank, account type and selected banking channel. Customers should confirm the applicable facilities and terms with the bank.

    Customers should review their statements regularly and report any transaction that appears incorrect or unfamiliar through the bank's official support channels.

    Project Information

    This Account Statements page is part of the Anish Souharda Cooperative Bank website project. It demonstrates how a cooperative bank can provide customers with clear information about accessing and managing their account transaction records.
